# Before public launch

This implementation minimises website data collection. It is not a certification of worldwide legal compliance. Legal obligations depend on company jurisdiction, target markets and actual processing; obtain a review appropriate to these facts before launch.

- Confirm the legal name, address, licence, TRN and address details from the PDF remain current, including the spelling “Naad Al Sheba”. Confirm licensing authority and whether additional business disclosures are required.
- Confirm the applicable UAE data protection regime (federal, DIFC, ADGM or other applicable rules) and whether EU/UK or other territorial rules apply to the business. Worldwide accessibility alone does not resolve territorial scope.
- Confirm rights to publish the illustration embedded in the company PDF. Permission to use the PDF as a design source does not establish its original image licence. Two Pexels photographs have been added; see image source and licence records in `docs/image-credits.md`.
- Confirm email/communications providers, recipient categories, processing locations, retention periods or meaningful criteria, legal bases and any required transfer safeguards. Finalise the privacy notice against actual practice; its current wording is a development draft, especially hosting and correspondence retention/transfer details.
- Confirm info@mina-consulting.com is active and the privacy contact can handle requests and identify the relevant supervisory authority and any legally required representative/contact details.
- GitHub Pages records visitor IP addresses for security. Avoid claims that no personal data is processed. Confirm relevant hosting terms, roles and safeguards, and replace the conditional GitHub hosting wording once deployed.
- No consent banner is included because the current site sets no cookies or similar storage and loads no third-party resources. Reassess before adding analytics, embeds, forms or advertising.
- Confirm the canonical domain, verify ownership and enable HTTPS. Check links, mobile layouts, keyboard navigation and contact details on the actual domain.
